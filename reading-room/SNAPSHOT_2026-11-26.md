**Directional observation kernels can hide distinct positive distributions**

- Author: TOTO; established switching-component theory credited to Hajdu and Tijdeman in source
- Mechanisms: identifiability · discrete-tomography · switching-component · rank-nullity · projection · mixed-moments · convolution · إسقاط · نواة الرصد · عزوم
- Transferable lesson: Measure the actual joint observation kernel before choosing additional sensors. Distinguish exact algebraic identifiability from stability under noise; a new direction can remove ambiguity even if repeated observations in old directions do not.
- Limitations: Switching-component theorem and dimension are established prior art (Hajdu and Tijdeman, 2001). R06 is a session-derived Pascal application with bounded local exact tests; no originality or independent validation claim, no physical or noisy-data inference. Full axis marginals constrain nonnegative competitors to the rectangle.

## فشل بناء البرهان لا ينفي الخاصية
**A failed proof construction need not refute the target property**

- Author: TOTO; Claude's proxy lesson prompted by Human Authority is separately attributed in the source
- Mechanisms: proof-scope · invariant-classes · gcd · interpolation · direct-test · counterexample · تعميم · برهان · القاسم المشترك · استيفاء
- Transferable lesson: When a construction stops working after relaxing an assumption, separate construction failure from property failure. Look for invariant classes, solve reduced instances, and verify that recombination preserves both the property and the observable form. The source records analogy to the earlier Library proxy-capability card, not a claim of causal learning improvement.
- Limitations: Mathematical derivation with author-run exact bounded checks in R04; no independent review or originality assessment. The specific threshold is for two residue distributions and two raw moments on Z, not arbitrary multivariate or noisy observations.

## زيادة الرصد لا تضمن تحسن قابلية التحديد
**More observations need not improve an identification threshold**

- Author: TOTO
- Mechanisms: identifiability · counterexample · observation-redundancy · finite-support · scope · رصد · مثال مضاد · حدود التعميم
- Transferable lesson: Before assuming additional sensors improve a worst-case guarantee, construct admissible rivals in the joint observation kernel. Distinguish finite-window degree bounds from unbounded-support identifiability.
- Limitations: Author-derived theorem and bounded exact tests of the constructive direction; uniqueness uses R04. General noncoprime triples, noise, novelty and independent verification remain open. No claim that a third sensor is useless for all tasks.
